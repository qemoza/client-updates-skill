# Example weekly report

Everything on this page is made up. The client, the work and the numbers are invented to show the shape.

The weekly report is the daily update with two or three numbers added. The numbers get one short paragraph right after the opener.

Run it with `/update Omar weekly`.

**The facts sheet**

```
Client: Omar. Covers: Monday to Sunday.
Call with him today: no

ALREADY SAID (he knows this, leave it out)
- The booking page is live (his channel, last week)

FACTS
- [accounts, Monday to Sunday] 38 trial classes booked. The week before: 29.
- [accounts, Monday to Sunday] 24 of the 38 came from the ad.
- [accounts, Monday to Sunday] Ad spend: 310 dollars.
- [accounts, worked out] 310 dollars divided by the 24 ad bookings is 12.92 dollars each.
- [board, finished] Waitlist for full classes is live. When a spot opens, the first person on the list gets a text.
- [board, in progress] The text that asks people to come back after their trial. Written. Still being tested.

WAITING ON THEM
- Ad budget for next week: keep it or raise it. Blocks next week's ad plan.

HELD BACK (real work the client does not need to read)
- Rebuilt the tracking so bookings are counted once.
- Clicks, views and the other ad numbers. They are on his dashboard.
```

**The report**

```
Hey Omar, here is the week.

From Monday to Sunday, 38 trial classes were booked, up from 29 the week before. 24 of those came from the ad, at 12.92 dollars each.

The waitlist for full classes is live. When a spot opens, the first person on the list gets a text.

We're now working on the text that asks people to come back after their trial. It is written and we're still testing it.

One thing we need from you. Do you want to keep the ad budget where it is next week, or raise it? We'll plan next week's ads once we know.
```

**What happened here**

- Three numbers, in one short paragraph, with the days they cover. Bookings, bookings from the ad, and the cost of each ad booking.
- Each number came from the account. None came from memory.
- The sum is shown on the facts sheet so a person can check it.
- Clicks, views and the total spend were left out. He decides from the cost of a booking.
- The rest is the normal update: what got finished, what is moving, the one ask.
